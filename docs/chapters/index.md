# Chapters

*Ole Cup Entrepreneurship: A Liberal Arts Guide to the St. Olaf Pitch Competition* is organized into 15 chapters covering 200 concepts. Chapters follow the Ole Cup application and competition cycle — from finding your idea to launching after competition day.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Ikigai and Self-Discovery](01-ikigai-and-self-discovery/index.md) — The Japanese concept of "reason for being" as the personal foundation for every venture idea.
2. [Ideation Across the Liberal Arts](02-ideation-across-liberal-arts/index.md) — Structured and unstructured techniques for generating venture ideas from any academic discipline.
3. [Recognizing Opportunity](03-recognizing-opportunity/index.md) — Distinguishing a genuine market opportunity from a mere idea through trend analysis and customer pain points.
4. [Value Propositions](04-value-propositions/index.md) — Crafting a clear, testable promise to customers using the Value Proposition Canvas and customer interviews.
5. [Minimum Viable Product Thinking](05-minimum-viable-product/index.md) — Testing your riskiest assumptions before building anything using the Lean Startup methodology.
6. [Social Ventures and Impact](06-social-ventures-and-impact/index.md) — The spectrum from nonprofit to for-profit and the hybrid models in between, including the Social Impact Prize.
7. [Team Formation and Roles](07-team-formation-and-roles/index.md) — Building a diverse founding team, assigning roles, and structuring co-founder agreements.
8. [Business Model Canvas](08-business-model-canvas/index.md) — The nine building blocks that show how every part of your venture fits together and makes money.
9. [Financial Fundamentals](09-financial-fundamentals/index.md) — Revenue models, unit economics, break-even analysis, and simple financial projections for student ventures.
10. [Marketing and Storytelling](10-marketing-and-storytelling/index.md) — Brand identity, the hero's journey, and zero-budget marketing channels for campus and beyond.
11. [The Pitch Deck](11-the-pitch-deck/index.md) — Market sizing, competitive analysis, and the complete six-slide pitch structure with delivery coaching.
12. [Ole Cup Process and Rules](12-ole-cup-process-and-rules/index.md) — The full Ole Cup lifecycle: application, mentoring phase, judging criteria, prizes, and the Minnesota Cup pathway.
13. [The St. Olaf Entrepreneurship Ecosystem](13-entrepreneurship-ecosystem/index.md) — Mentors, grants, and partner organizations — plus advanced discovery tools to use with them.
14. [Learning from Failure](14-learning-from-failure/index.md) — Pivots, post-mortems, and the growth mindset tools that turn setbacks into smarter next steps.
15. [From Pitch to Launch](15-from-pitch-to-launch/index.md) — Post-competition action steps, founder agreements, revenue model choices, and Ole Cup alumni launch stories.

## How to Use This Textbook

Start at Chapter 1 and work through in order — each chapter's concepts build on what came before. Students applying for the Ole Cup should complete Chapters 1–11 before the February 15 application deadline, then use Chapters 12–15 during the mentoring phase and after competition day. Every chapter includes a "Try It" exercise section and an "Ole Cup Connection" box that maps the content directly to your application materials.
