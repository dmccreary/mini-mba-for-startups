# Character Sheet: Rune the Raven

The canonical identity document for Rune, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Ole Cup Entrepreneurship: A Liberal Arts Guide to the St. Olaf Pitch Competition** textbook.
Every pose prompt and every piece of AI-generated content involving this character
must re-anchor to the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Rune
- **Species:** Raven
- **Subject:** Entrepreneurship (Liberal Arts)
- **Catchphrase:** "Your Ikigai is waiting — let's find it!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Deep midnight-black feathers with a warm amber-gold sheen on the wings and chest — hex `#1a1a2e` body, `#f59e0b` amber highlights
- **Accent color:** St. Olaf gold/amber — hex `#f59e0b`
- **Clothing / accessories:** A small amber-and-black knit scarf, a tiny rectangular name badge that reads "Ole Cup", bright curious eyes with amber irises, slightly oversized feet that give a grounded, ready-to-leap look
- **Expression:** Alert and optimistic — beak slightly open in an encouraging half-smile, eyes wide and forward-focused
- **Size proportion:** Compact and upright — stands about 1.3× the height of the name badge, suitable for icon-sized display at 90px
- **Art style:** Flat cartoon / vector illustration, clean bold lines, transparent background

## Personality

- **Curious** — always leaning forward, spotting connections others miss
- **Encouraging** — genuinely celebrates every student's effort and idea
- **Bold** — unafraid of new ideas, models the courage to pitch
- **Grounded** — rooted in Norse heritage and St. Olaf's liberal arts values; wisdom over hype

## Voice

- Warm and conversational — speaks like a slightly older peer who has pitched before and survived
- Uses simple, direct language — no MBA jargon, no buzzwords
- Occasionally weaves in Norse or St. Olaf references for warmth
- Signature phrases: "Your Ikigai is waiting — let's find it!", "Every founder started exactly where you are.", "Ideas are cheap — curiosity is priceless."

## Why This Mascot

Ravens appear in Norse mythology as Hugin and Munin — Odin's ravens representing *Thought* and *Memory* — making Rune a natural fit for St. Olaf College's Norwegian heritage. As a raven, Rune embodies the entrepreneurial skill of seeing patterns and opportunities that others overlook, a quality prized across every liberal arts discipline from music to science. The amber-gold coloring echoes St. Olaf's school colors and the Ole Cup competition's warm trophy aesthetic.
